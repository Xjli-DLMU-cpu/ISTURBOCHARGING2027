WELCOME TO INTERNATIONAL SEMINAR OF TURBOCHARGING 2027!

The International Seminar of Turbocharging 2027 will be held on Thursday–Friday, 19–20 August 2027 in Dalian, China.

The Turbocharging Seminar is an established international seminar series dedicated to engine turbocharging and turbomachinery technologies. Launched in 2013, the seminar has been held annually with one interruption. This will be the 14th edition of the series.

ISTURBOCHARGING 2027 now calls for papers and oral presentations. The topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Internal combustion engine technologies
  • Turbocharging and turbocharger technologies
  • Turbomachinery technologies and emerging applications
  • Artificial intelligence applied in turbomachinery and power machinery
  • Advanced design methods for turbomachinery
  • Computational and experimental fluid dynamics for turbomachinery and powertrain systems

Abstracts of 150–300 words in English shall be submitted to:
xjli@dlmu.edu.cn and huachen204887@163.com.
