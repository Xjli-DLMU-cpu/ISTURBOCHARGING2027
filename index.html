WELCOME TO INTERNATIONAL SEMINAR OF TURBOCHARGING 2027!

The International Seminar of Turbocharging 2027 will be held on Thursday–Friday, 19–20 August 2027 in Dalian, China.

The Turbocharging Seminar is an established international seminar series dedicated to engine turbocharging and turbomachinery technologies. Launched in 2013, the seminar has been held annually with one interruption. This will be the 14th edition of the series.
