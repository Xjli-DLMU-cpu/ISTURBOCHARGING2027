Submission Instructions:

Submitted papers must be neither previously published nor under review by another workshop, conference or journal.

Only electronic submissions in PDF will be accepted. Submitted papers must be written in English, must render without error using standard PDF viewing tools, and must include the author names and affiliations on the first page.

Papers should use the standard ISTC Template, available at ACM Article Template .

To submit your paper, please follow below link to the submission site:

Paper Submission Site

Note that at least one of the authors of each paper accepted for presentation in ISTC 2027 must be registered.
