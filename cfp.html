Call For Papers (PDF,TXT,BROCHURE)

ISTURBOCHARGING 2027 now calls for papers and oral presentations. The topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Internal combustion engine technologies
  • Turbocharging and turbocharger technologies
  • Turbomachinery technologies and emerging applications
  • Artificial intelligence applied in turbomachinery and power machinery
  • Advanced design methods for turbomachinery
  • Computational and experimental fluid dynamics for turbomachinery and powertrain systems

Abstracts of 150–300 words in English shall be submitted:See the submission page.
